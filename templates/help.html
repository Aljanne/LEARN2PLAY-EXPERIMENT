Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Learn2Play as a student, gamer, or mentor.

Unlike passive YouTube videos where nobody can correct your specific mistakes, Learn2Play gives you live, personalized 1-on-1 mentorship. Compared to generic freelance marketplaces like Fiverr, Learn2Play is:

  • Dual-specialized: Tailored specifically for academic subjects (Math, Science, Languages) AND competitive gaming (Valorant, MLBB, Dota 2, LoL).
  • Credential-verified: Every teacher and coach submits diplomas, certificates, or competitive rank screenshots before coaching.
  • Flexible & local: Sessions are scheduled around your availability, with support for students and gamers starting in Naga City.

We are currently piloting Learn2Play specifically targeting students, gamers, and educators residing in Naga City. This allows us to work closely with local schools, esports teams, and university communities.

We are planning to expand to more regions across the Philippines soon as our mentor network scales!

Learn2Play charges a 20% platform commission from every coaching session booked. The remaining 80% is paid directly to the mentor.

Example: If you set your hourly lesson rate at ₱300:

  • You receive ₱240 directly into your payout balance.
  • Learn2Play retains ₱60 to cover platform hosting, security, credential verification, and customer support.

To ensure student safety and high instructional quality, applications undergo admin review:

  • Academic Teachers: Submit a photo or PDF of your college diploma, PRC license, certificate of enrollment, or official academic transcript.
  • Gaming Coaches: Submit proof of competitive tier/rank (e.g. Immortal/Radiant in Valorant, Mythical Immortal in MLBB), tournament placement certificates, or coaching portfolio links.

Review takes approximately 24 to 48 hours. In the meantime, you can use your account as a student.

Sessions are conducted online using voice/video and screen-sharing tools (such as Discord, Google Meet, or Zoom). For gamers, mentors will do live VOD reviews, custom match analysis, and mechanic training drills.

If a coach or teacher fails to attend without a 24-hour advance notice, you are entitled to a full reschedule or credit refund. You can also report no-shows directly from your My Sessions page.
